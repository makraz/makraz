Most projects that fail do not break down: they slowly become impossible to change. A feature that took two days takes ten, every fix breaks something else, and the team spends more time working around the existing code than building on it. The cost never appears on the first invoice — it arrives in year two, exactly when the business needs to accelerate.

We build the other way round. A boring, documented architecture, proven frameworks rather than fashionable ones, tests where a mistake is expensive, and releases frequent enough that you watch the product move every week. Less spectacular at the start, and far cheaper to live with afterwards.
