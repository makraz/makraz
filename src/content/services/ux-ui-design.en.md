Bad design is not always visible: it shows up as support calls, abandoned forms and features nobody finds. The cause is rarely taste. It is decisions made without watching how people actually use the product — navigation modelled on the org chart, an important button weighted like six others, labels written by people who already know the business.

So we start from the tasks your users came to complete, and settle the structure in grey before drawing anything. What comes out is not a set of images but a system your teams reuse: the product stays coherent as it grows, and each new screen costs less than the last.
