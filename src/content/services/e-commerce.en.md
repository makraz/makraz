Selling online rarely fails at the payment step. It fails at the basket: the customer cannot tell what delivery will cost until the last step, the product page does not say which of the four sizes they need, and nothing indicates when the order will arrive. Every uncertainty left with the customer is an order that does not happen.

So we build the store around the questions that come before the purchase, in the order people ask them: which one do I choose, what does it cost delivered, when do I get it, and what happens if I got it wrong. The back office follows the same logic — usable by your team on day one, without training.
