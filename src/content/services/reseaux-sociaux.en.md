Most social presences die the same way: an enthusiastic start, five posts a week, then a slowdown, then an account whose last post is eight months old. That abandoned account now says something about you — often worse than having no account at all.

So we build for the rhythm you can sustain, not the one that impresses at launch. A written editorial line, a calendar prepared in advance, two platforms rather than five, and honest measurement of what it actually returns in contacts. The day you want to take it back in-house, everything is documented so that you can.
