Motion often arrives at the end of a project as decoration: the logo gets animated because it can be, transitions get added because the tool offers them. The result is expensive, slows pages down, and gains nothing — while the video that would genuinely have helped, the one explaining in thirty seconds what the product does, never got made.

So we start from intent: what this animation has to achieve in the person watching it. The script is agreed before any production, because that is where corrections are free. And we leave templates behind, so your routine posts do not depend on calling an agency.
