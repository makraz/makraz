There comes a point where the problem is no longer the number of developers. Releases slip without anyone able to say why, every estimate is contradicted, and architecture decisions get made by default — under pressure, by whoever was available that day. Meanwhile the founder has no way to check the answers they are given.

That is a leadership vacuum, not a shortage of skill. We take that role part-time: decide, write the priorities down, say no when it is needed, hire in the right order, and translate all of it into something your partners can act on. You keep the decision; you stop making it alone and uninformed.
