Plenty of companies invest months in a product, then a few days in how anyone will hear about it. The site is live, it is better than the competition's, and nobody finds it: no page answers the questions customers actually type, the ads point at a page that does not promise what the ad promised, and the content stops after three articles.

We treat visibility as part of the product rather than a layer added afterwards. It starts with the words your customers use — usually not the ones you use — then the pages that answer them, and only then the media budget. Every action is measured against one thing: qualified enquiries, not impressions.
