Hiring a product team takes months: four or five hires, scarce profiles, and the need to know upfront what the product will require a year from now. So many companies do the opposite — a developer here, a freelancer there, a designer for three weeks — and discover that nobody answers for the result as a whole.

A dedicated team solves that without a hiring commitment: people who already work together, one accountable lead, and capacity that follows the product's phase. We document continuously so bringing it in-house stays possible at any point — that is a successful ending, not a lost account.
