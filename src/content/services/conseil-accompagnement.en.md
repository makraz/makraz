The most expensive decisions in communication and technology are often made unchallenged: a quote nobody in the room can evaluate, a platform chosen because an acquaintance recommended it, a senior hire decided on an impression. The cost does not show up immediately — it shows up a year later, when it all has to be redone.

A short outside opinion corrects that for a fraction of the price of the mistake. We look at the actual material, ask the questions the supplier did not, and give a recommendation with its risks owned. Including when that recommendation is to launch nothing and solve the problem another way.
