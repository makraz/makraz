A mobile app rarely fails during development. It fails at submission, or six months later: the listing is rejected over an incomplete privacy policy, the Android version trails iOS by a year, and nobody anticipated that the next OS update would force a rewrite of the authentication code.

So we treat release and lifespan as part of the project rather than a closing formality. That starts with an honest scope — two or three things we intend to be flawless on a phone — and ends with a maintenance arrangement that follows Apple's and Google's annual cycles.
