Many companies discover SEO after launch, when the traffic does not arrive. That is the worst moment: the URL structure is fixed, pages were conceived as an internal catalogue rather than as answers to questions, and fixing it means rebuilding part of the site. Search visibility is not a layer you add — it is a consequence of how the site was structured.

So we start with the technical audit, because an indexing blocker cancels all the editorial work that follows. Then we build page by page, on your customers' real search intents. And we measure what counts: not impressions, but the qualified enquiries that land in your inbox.
