"We should be on social media" is almost always the symptom of a skipped question: to say what, to whom, and why you rather than someone else. With no answer, production starts anyway — posts, a blog, maybe a campaign — and every piece of content re-asks the positioning question without ever settling it.

So we make the decisions first, and write them down briefly. What you are, for whom, in what voice, on which channels, and above all what you are choosing not to do. The deliverable is a few usable pages rather than a deck nobody reopens — and it stays valid even if you execute it without us.
