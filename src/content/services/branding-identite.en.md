An identity rarely fails on presentation day. It fails six months later: three versions of the logo are in circulation, everyone picks their own colours, sales documents no longer resemble the website, and the brand becomes a collection of approximations. The problem was never the logo, but the absence of rules usable by the people producing materials every day.

So we deliver an identity built to be applied by people other than us: written rules with examples, the applications that actually get used, and every source file in your hands. The measure of success is not the initial presentation but the state of the brand two years later.
