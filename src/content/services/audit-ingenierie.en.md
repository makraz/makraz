Engineering problems rarely announce themselves clearly. They show up as unexplained delays, contradicted estimates, incidents whose cause stays vague — and a non-technical leader has no way to tell whether it is the team, the tooling, the organisation or accumulated debt. Asking the existing team to diagnose itself puts everyone in an impossible position.

A short external audit supplies what is missing: a view with nothing to defend. We read the code and the infrastructure, we talk to the people doing the work, and we hand back written findings without jargon, with ranked priorities and a plan you can execute with us or without us.
