Bad web copy is not recognisable by its mistakes but by its absence of a point of view: sentences that could sit on any competitor's site, interchangeable promises, and a reader who closes the tab having learned nothing. That copy does not only cost its price — it occupies the space of the copy that would have convinced.

So we start with an interview with the people who actually speak to customers, because the good phrasing already exists inside your company. The text that comes out takes a position, uses your customers' words, and works in all three languages because it is rewritten in each rather than translated from one.
