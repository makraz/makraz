Most MVPs that find their market get rewritten within eighteen months — exactly when the business should be accelerating. The cause is not having started small: it is confusing "reduced scope" with "sloppy work". Tests get skipped, shortcuts pile up in the data model, and the debt is taken on before the first user arrives.

We do the opposite: we cut scope, never the quality of what gets built. A clear hypothesis, the smallest thing that can test it, and a boring architecture that will carry what comes next. What ships in a few weeks then remains a foundation you can build on, rather than a prototype to throw away.
