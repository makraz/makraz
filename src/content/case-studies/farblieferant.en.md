## The context

[To validate with the client] Farblieferant sells paints and supplies to professionals and consumers in Germany. The need: moving from traditional sales to an online platform able to handle a large catalog, professional pricing and recurring orders — at German-market standards.

## The solution

[To validate with the client] A custom store: a catalog structured by ranges and uses, secure online payment, professional accounts with dedicated pricing, and an architecture built for growth. SEO was designed in from day one for the German-speaking market.
